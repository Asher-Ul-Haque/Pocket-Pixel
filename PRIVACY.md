# Privacy Policy
_Last updated: August 13, 2025_


Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how **Pixel Pocket (the "App")** handles your information.

1. Information Collection and Use
The App is a Game Boy emulator designed to play games and manage save files that you provide. **I do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or usage data from you. The App is designed to operate without collecting any information that could be used to identify you personally**.

2. User-Provided Content
**All game ROMs and save files used by the App are files that you, the user, provide. These files are stored locally on your device only**. The App does not upload, transmit, or share these files with us or any third parties. You have full control over these files and can delete them at any time from your device's storage.

3. Data Storage and Cache
The App uses your device's local storage for its primary function (storing ROMs and save files). Additionally, the App uses a local cache to store box art for the game cartridges. This cached box art is also **stored locally on your device only and is not shared with us or any third parties**.

4. Third-Party Services
The App does not use any third-party services, analytics, or advertising that would collect or track your data.

5. FOSS
The App is **free and open source** and will stay that way. The app can be compiled directly from the source code in this repository. It will match the one on google playstore.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
I may update my Privacy Policy from time to time, but I don't even plan on it. I will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
